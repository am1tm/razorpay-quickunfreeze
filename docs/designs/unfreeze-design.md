## Problem Statement
Razorpay SME merchants lose access to their money with no warning, no reason, and no one to talk to. Across 887 Play Store reviews since Jan 2025 and ~110 merchant accounts on X, the three loudest merchant complaints are **support unreachable (18%)**, **KYC/onboarding rejected without a reason (18%, most upvoted)** and **account disabled / funds held (12%)**. The most-upvoted review in the dataset (266 👍) is a hold story. The RBI re-KYC deadline (reportedly moved to 15 Mar 2027) means Razorpay will pause settlements about 30 days early (per [Razorpay's re-KYC docs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/dashboard/re-kyc/), which say settlements go on hold 30 days before the deadline; *assumption: the rule carries over to the extended date*), around **13 Feb 2027**, for any merchant who hasn't re-KYC'd. Today re-KYC is a desktop-dashboard flow, and held merchants are told to "contact support".

Build Challenge brief (FSB track): a mobile experience in which an SME merchant completes ONE urgent / frequent / high-value task end to end, ideally in under 2 minutes. Deliverables: one-page product note, 90-second video, working prototype, AI build log. Deadline: **13 Oct 2026, 11:59 PM IST**.

## What Makes This Cool
- **The ice-cube:** the merchant's money appears as a frozen block. In the prevention flow the ice **forms** as the deadline approaches. Each requirement cleared **melts** it, with a haptic tick, and the amount counts back up as it thaws.
- **Money first, not compliance first:** every other hold experience (Razorpay, Shopify Payments) is a red banner plus a form plus a ticket. Here the main element on screen is the ₹ amount and the date, and the KYC steps are how the merchant keeps it.
- **Ask out loud, in your own language:** the merchant taps 🎙️ and says *"mera paisa kyun rukega?"* ("why will my money be held?") or the same in Marathi or Tamil. The answer comes back **spoken in that language**, using Sarvam AI's Indian-language speech models. This suits a merchant at the counter with their hands full, and makes for a memorable moment in the video.
- **No physical PAN card:** PAN and Aadhaar come through DigiLocker consent, which is legally equivalent to the originals. The camera is used only for a shop-front photo. This removes a complaint we saw on both X and the Play Store.

## Recommended Approach
**Flutter app plus one Vercel serverless function for AI (Gemini free tier by default).**

**Screens** (wireframe: `docs/design/unfreeze-wireframe.html` / `.png`):
1. **Home**
   - Greeting and business name.
   - **Ice hero card:** "₹48,200", "Settlements freeze on 13 Feb", a "123 days left" chip and a **Stop the freeze** button. Frost creeps in from the edges.
   - **Risk-hold teaser card**, already frozen, with the accrual line.
   - Today's collections card.
   - Bottom navigation: Home, Payments, Settlements, Account. Payments and Settlements are static lists with believable mock data.
2. **Stop the freeze flow**
   - A smaller ice block at the top that melts by thirds.
   - An AI "Why this is happening" card with an EN/हिं toggle (**AI call 1**; Gemini by default, Claude optional).
   - A three-step checklist:
     1. Confirm business details. They're pre-filled; the merchant just confirms.
     2. PAN and Aadhaar through a **mock DigiLocker** screen: Aadhaar-linked mobile, OTP, consent, then "fetched ✓".
     3. **Shop-front photo** through `image_picker`, checked by **AI call 2 (Gemini vision, free tier)**. The result is pass, or a retake with a specific reason ("too dark, shop name not readable").
3. **Freeze stopped**
   - The ice is gone and the screen reads **"Submitted ✓ Your settlements won't pause while we review"**. It doesn't promise approval.
   - A reference number and an honest tracker (Submitted, Under review usually within 24h, Verified by push).
   - "Book a 30-min verification visit" (the RBI requires in-person verification by PA employees).
   - "Share confirmation on WhatsApp".
   - "Talk to a person (case pre-filled)", which addresses the top complaint.
4. **Risk-hold teaser (about 5 seconds in the video)**
   - Tapping the card opens an already-frozen block with accrual cubes.
   - "Add proof of delivery" picks 2 gallery images, and the block melts.
   - This is mock only, with no AI call, unless there's time left.

**Entry:** on Android, a local notification (`flutter_local_notifications`) fires a few seconds after launch and opens Home with the ice card focused. There is no login screen; the prototype assumes a remembered device or biometric login. On web, an in-app notification banner simulates it.

**Ice-cube rendering:** a `CustomPainter` draws a rounded translucent block with a frost gradient, a crack overlay and a frost rim driven by `progress` (0 is frozen, 1 is melted), animated with an `AnimationController` plus a `TweenAnimationBuilder` for the ₹ count-up. A drip and shrink effect is added on each step, with `HapticFeedback.mediumImpact()` on every melt. A fragment shader is optional polish. Build the painter first so it never blocks.

**AI proxy (provider-agnostic):** a Vercel serverless function `/api/ai` with three modes, all using structured JSON output:
- `explain {lang}` returns `{text, lang}` (the default "why" explanation).
- `ask {question, lang}` returns `{text, lang}` (an answer to a spoken or typed question, ≤ 2 short sentences).
- `photo_check {image_jpeg_base64}` returns `{verdict: pass|retake, reason}`.

Supported `lang`: `en`, `hi`, `mr`. Every mode has a canned fallback per language: the explanation text for `explain` and `ask`, and an advisory pass for `photo_check` (see the edge cases section).
- **Default provider:** Google **Gemini API free tier** (a Flash vision model, key from Google AI Studio). Free-tier rate limits are fine for a demo. Free-tier inputs may be used by Google to improve its models, so only use demo photos, never real KYC documents.
- An env var `AI_PROVIDER=gemini|claude` keeps Claude available as a drop-in for `explain` if desired.
- Keys stay in Vercel environment variables and never ship in the APK. Check the current free Flash model name in AI Studio before coding. **Every call has a timeout and a canned fallback** (6 s for `explain` and `photo_check`; voice stages use the budget in the build contracts), so the demo can't break on bad Wi-Fi.

**State:** a single state machine (`FreezeCase {status, stepsDone, amount, deadline}`) drives both the painter progress and the checklist. Data comes from local JSON fixtures.

**Voice (Sarvam AI):**
- **Flow:** 🎙️ press-and-hold on the "Why this is happening" card. Record with the `record` package (Android mic permission; browser mic on web). Send to `/api/voice?mode=stt`, which calls **Saaras v3** in `transcribe` mode and detects the language. That text goes to AI call 1 with "answer in <lang>, ≤ 2 short sentences". `/api/voice?mode=tts` then calls **Bulbul v3** with a voice for that language, and the app plays the returned audio with `just_audio`.
- **Languages for the demo:** Hindi (main), Marathi (Bharti is in Pune, so this is the video moment), and English. Show a language chip on the card.
- **Fallback:** pre-generate one default answer clip per language (हिं / मराठी / EN) into the app's assets. Per the build contracts, if any voice stage exceeds its budget or fails, play the clip for the selected language chip and show the canned text. The video should never depend on live network.
- **Cost:** about 20–50 demo runs × ~300 characters of TTS plus a few minutes of speech-to-text is a few rupees, well within the ₹1,000 credit. Check the current model names and voice IDs in the Sarvam docs before coding, because naming has changed (Saarika → Saaras).

**Indian-language text:** bundle Noto Sans Devanagari (Hindi and Marathi) so the web build renders without fetching fonts.

## Build contracts and edge cases (from spec review)
- **Photo upload size:** `image_picker(maxWidth: 1280, imageQuality: 70)` produces JPEGs under ~400 KB. The proxy rejects bodies over 2 MB with HTTP 413, and the app treats that like a failed call (advisory pass below).
- **Photo-check fallback:** on a timeout (6 s), an error or a 413, the step shows **"Photo accepted. Automatic quality check unavailable; a reviewer will check it."** and lets the merchant continue.
- **AI advises, never blocks:** after the first `retake`, a **"Submit anyway, a person will review"** link appears next to Retake. AI verdicts never gate submission (premise 5).
- **Tracker failure state:** if the mocked review comes back "needs one more thing", the tracker shows that step in amber. Tapping it reopens the checklist with that item unchecked and a sliver of ice re-forming. A demo toggle in debug settings can trigger this.
- **Voice time budget:** **9 s from releasing the mic to audio start**, split STT ≤ 2.5 s, `ask` ≤ 3 s, TTS ≤ 3.5 s. *(TTS raised from 2.5 s to 3.5 s by the owner on 2026-10-12 after live Bulbul v3 measured 3.0 s.)* If any stage hits its limit or errors, stop the chain and play the **bundled clip for the selected language chip**, with its canned text shown.
- **Voice fallback selection:** the language chip (हिं / मराठी / EN) is always selected (default हिं) and is the source of truth for answer and TTS language. It **overrides** the detected language (Hindi vs Marathi detection is unreliable on short phrases). Each language has 1 bundled "why is my money held" answer clip, plus 3 tappable suggested-question chips that work without the mic. If STT succeeds but `ask` fails, play the language's default clip.
- **Audio formats:** record **WAV, 16 kHz mono PCM** with `record` (`AudioEncoder.wav`) on both Android and web. Saaras accepts WAV, so no conversion is needed. `/api/voice?mode=tts` returns `{audio_base64, mime: "audio/wav"}`, and the app plays it with `just_audio` from `Uri.dataFromBytes(bytes, mimeType: 'audio/wav')`. That works on Android and web alike.
- **Permissions (Android 13+):** request `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` on first launch. If it's denied, show the in-app freeze banner instead of a system notification, the same path as on web. Request `RECORD_AUDIO` on the first mic tap. If it's denied, hide the mic and leave the suggested-question chips and the language chip clips.
- **Third-party AI notice:** the photo step shows **"Prototype: photos are sent to a third-party AI (Google Gemini) for a quality check. Don't upload real documents."** The mic shows a similar one-line note for Sarvam.
