QuickUnfreeze for Razorpay
Product Note · Amit Mishra
Prototype: rzp-unfreeze.vercel.app
Android: rzp-unfreeze.vercel.app/unfreeze.apk
"My money stopped and nobody told me why" is the loudest complaint in Razorpay's Play Store reviews, across holds and KYC blocks. QuickUnfreeze turns it into a 90-second fix on the phone, starting with re-KYC.
1Who the merchant is
A no-code micro merchant who runs the business from one phone.
  • Owner-run, 1–10 people, Payment Links or QR, Tier 1–3, ~₹0.5–5 lakh a month; local vernacular first.
  • Settlements are their working capital; MSMEs already have ₹8.1 lakh crore stuck in delayed payments1.
  • Bharti, the baker in Nagpur: "Tell me early, in my language, and let me fix it between orders."
2Today & the friction
She finds out from a missing bank credit, with no reason and a desktop-only fix.

Re-KYC holds start ~13 Feb*; 30–35% of small merchants may miss it2.

3Why mobile
Every step of the fix is native to the phone; the laptop route is the problem.
  • 71% of MSMEs (84% of women entrepreneurs) run the business mainly on a smartphone3.
  • Push reaches her months ahead, on the phone she checks all day.
  • DigiLocker OTP lands on the same phone and fills itself in.
  • Camera checks the one photo before it's sent.
  • Voice: she asks in Hindi or Marathi and hears the answer.

QuickUnfreeze, via R.A.Y., answers her by voice in Hindi or Marathi, knows her case (why this hold, what's needed) and handles follow-up "why" questions, right inside the flow.

₹88,250 (a week of settlements) never goes on hold. Same pattern for a risk hold: proof from the gallery.

5Decisions & what I didn't build
Warn early, resolve fast; AI advises, a person decides.
  • One pattern for every hold; AI only for R.A.Y. answers and the photo check.
What I didn't build, and why
✕AI releasing holds or approving KYC → money and compliance need a human.
✕R.A.Y. in a side panel or chat → it sits inside the hold flow, in context.
✕AI blocking the photo → after one retake she can "submit anyway"; a person reviews.
✕Exact fraud-check triggers → they'd teach fraudsters to dodge the checks; she sees a plain reason ("orders larger than usual").
